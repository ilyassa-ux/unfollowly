# Unfollowly Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026

Unfollowly processes Instagram follower and following export files locally on the user's device. The app does not require an Instagram password, create an account, transmit imported relationship data to a developer-operated server, use advertising identifiers, or sell personal data.

Imported snapshots are stored in the app's private local storage and excluded from Android cloud backup. Users can delete all imported data from Settings or by clearing/uninstalling the app.

When a user chooses **View**, Android opens the public Instagram profile URL in an external app or browser. That service applies its own privacy policy.

Unfollowly is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.
